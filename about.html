ABOUT JEEVANJAGRUK

Health awareness should be clear, accessible, and shared.

JeevanJagruk is a public-health awareness project created to make important health topics easier to understand and explore. Through learning resources and awareness initiatives, we aim to support informed choices and a more prepared community.

Explore Our Projects Get Involved

Why JeevanJagruk?

Health information can feel complicated or difficult to navigate. JeevanJagruk aims to present general awareness topics in approachable language and help visitors find credible resources for further learning.

Our Approach

Accessible Learning

Explain health-awareness topics in clear language that is easier for a broad audience to follow.

Credible Resources

Encourage learning from reliable sources and make the limits of general awareness information clear.

Community Awareness

Share useful information, encourage preparedness, and support appropriate help-seeking and recognized practical training.

Our Current Focus

One of our learning initiatives is Basic Life Support Awareness. It introduces emergency recognition, getting help, and the importance of learning CPR and AED skills through recognized practical training.
