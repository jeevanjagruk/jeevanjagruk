How to Check Health Information

  • Check the source: Is the information from a qualified health organization or professional?
  • Check the date: Is it current, or could the guidance have changed?
  • Look for evidence: Does it explain where its claims come from?
  • Compare sources: Do other reliable health sources support the same information?
  • Be cautious with promises: Be skeptical of claims that guarantee a cure or ask you to stop prescribed treatment.
Important: This website provides general educational awareness. It does not replace professional medical advice, emergency care, or recognized practical training. In an emergency, contact local emergency services and follow their instructions.
