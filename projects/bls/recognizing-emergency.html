Basic Life Support Awareness

Recognizing an Emergency

Recognizing that someone may need urgent medical help is an important first step. If you think a situation may be an emergency, seek professional assistance promptly.

Possible warning signs

Medical emergencies can have different causes and may not always look the same. Possible warning signs include:

  • Unresponsiveness or sudden collapse
  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Sudden chest pain or pressure
  • Sudden weakness, confusion, or difficulty speaking
  • Severe bleeding or a serious injury
  • A seizure, particularly if it is prolonged or the person does not recover as expected

This list is not complete and cannot diagnose a condition. Do not delay seeking help because a symptom is not listed here.

Getting help

Notice what is happening

Look for signs that the person may need urgent medical assistance.

Contact emergency services

Call your local emergency number promptly and clearly explain the situation.

Follow professional guidance

Follow the dispatcher’s instructions and provide assistance within your training.

Remember

This page is for general awareness, not diagnosis or treatment. If you believe someone may be experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately and follow their guidance.
