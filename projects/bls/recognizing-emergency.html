Basic Life Support Awareness

Recognizing an Emergency

Recognizing that someone may need urgent medical help is an important first step. When in doubt, seek professional assistance promptly.

Possible warning signs

A medical emergency can have many causes and may not always look the same. Warning signs can include:

  • Unresponsiveness or sudden collapse
  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Sudden chest pain or pressure
  • Sudden weakness, confusion, or difficulty speaking
  • Severe bleeding or a serious injury
  • Seizure, especially if it is prolonged or the person does not recover normally

This list is not complete. Do not delay seeking help because a symptom is not listed here.

What should you do?

Notice the situation

Pay attention to what is happening and whether the person may need urgent help.

Get emergency help

Contact local emergency services promptly and follow the instructions provided.

Stay within your training

Follow the emergency dispatcher’s guidance and provide only assistance you are trained to give.

Remember

This page is for general awareness and is not a diagnostic tool. If you believe someone may be experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
