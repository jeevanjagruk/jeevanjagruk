AED Awareness

Understanding what an automated external defibrillator is and why it can matter in an emergency.

Important: This page is for general awareness only. It is not a substitute for certified, hands-on training or emergency dispatcher instructions. In an emergency, contact local emergency services and follow the dispatcher’s guidance.

What is an AED?

An automated external defibrillator (AED) is a portable device that can check a person’s heart rhythm and, when appropriate, deliver an electrical shock. AEDs are designed to provide voice or visual instructions to guide a user.

Why AED awareness matters

In some sudden cardiac emergencies, rapid access to an AED may help improve the chance of survival. Knowing what an AED is and where one may be located can help people respond more confidently while emergency services are being contacted.

Where might AEDs be found?

AEDs may be available in places such as airports, shopping centres, sports facilities, workplaces, and other public buildings. Their availability and placement vary by location.

Learn through practical training

This page does not provide instructions for operating an AED. Consider learning from a recognized first-aid or basic life support training provider. During an emergency, follow the device’s prompts and the instructions given by emergency services.
