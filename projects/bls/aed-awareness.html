AED Awareness

Learn what an automated external defibrillator (AED) is, where one might be available, and why following emergency guidance matters.

Important: This page is for general awareness only. It does not replace practical training or emergency dispatcher instructions. In an emergency, contact local emergency services promptly and follow their guidance.

What is an AED?

An automated external defibrillator (AED) is a portable device that analyses a person’s heart rhythm and, when appropriate, advises a shock. AEDs provide voice or visual prompts to guide a user.

Why AED awareness matters

In some cardiac emergencies, rapid access to an AED may be important. Knowing what the device is and how to locate one can help people understand the resources that may be available while emergency services are being contacted.

Where might AEDs be found?

AEDs may be available in places such as airports, shopping centres, sports facilities, workplaces, and other public buildings. Their availability and placement vary by location.

Learn through practical training

This page does not provide instructions for operating an AED. Consider learning through a recognized, hands-on first-aid or BLS course. In an emergency, follow the device’s prompts and the instructions given by emergency services.
