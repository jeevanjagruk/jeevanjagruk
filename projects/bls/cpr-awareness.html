CPR Awareness

Cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) is an emergency response skill used when a person’s heart has stopped beating effectively. Prompt emergency help and trained response can be critical.

What is CPR?

CPR combines actions intended to help maintain blood flow and support a person experiencing cardiac arrest until further care is available. The appropriate response depends on the situation and the rescuer’s training.

Why learn CPR?

Recognize the need for help

Learn to identify a possible emergency and contact emergency services promptly.

Build practical skills

Hands-on instruction helps learners practise skills with qualified guidance.

Be prepared

Training can help people understand how to respond and follow emergency guidance.

Learn from qualified instructors

This page provides general awareness, not CPR instructions or certification. CPR techniques and recommendations can vary by age and circumstance. Attend a recognized, hands-on course and follow emergency dispatcher instructions during an emergency.
