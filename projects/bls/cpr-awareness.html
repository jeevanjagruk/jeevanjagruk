CPR Awareness

Cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) is an emergency response skill used when someone may be experiencing cardiac arrest. Prompt emergency assistance and an appropriate response can be critical.

What is CPR?

CPR involves actions intended to help maintain blood flow and support a person experiencing cardiac arrest until further medical care is available. The appropriate response depends on the person’s age, circumstances, and the responder’s training.

Why learn CPR?

Recognize a possible emergency

Learn to recognize when urgent help may be needed and contact emergency services promptly.

Practise with guidance

Hands-on instruction gives learners an opportunity to practise skills with a qualified instructor.

Understand how to get help

Training can help learners understand their role and how to follow emergency guidance.

Learn through recognized training

This page provides general awareness only. It does not provide step-by-step CPR instruction or certification. CPR recommendations can differ by age and situation. Learn practical skills through a recognized, hands-on course and follow emergency dispatcher instructions during an emergency.
