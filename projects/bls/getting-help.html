Basic Life Support Awareness

Getting Help

In a possible medical emergency, contacting emergency services promptly and following their instructions are important steps.

When you contact emergency services

Share what you know as clearly as you can. The dispatcher may ask for:

  • Your location and a way to contact you
  • What happened and what you can observe
  • How many people need help
  • Whether the person is responsive and breathing normally, if you can safely tell

Stay on the line and follow the dispatcher’s instructions. If you do not know an answer, say so rather than guessing.

While waiting for help

Keep yourself safe

Do not enter an unsafe area. Follow instructions from emergency services.

Follow guidance

Listen carefully and follow the dispatcher’s instructions to the best of your ability.

Share updates

Tell the dispatcher if the situation changes or new information becomes available.

Remember

Emergency procedures and contact numbers can vary by location. Know how to reach local emergency services where you live. This page is for general awareness and does not replace professional instruction or emergency dispatcher guidance.
