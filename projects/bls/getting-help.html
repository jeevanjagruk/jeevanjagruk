Basic Life Support Awareness

Getting Help

In a possible medical emergency, contact local emergency services promptly. Share what you know clearly and follow the dispatcher’s instructions.

What information may be helpful?

The dispatcher may ask you for details such as:

  • Your location, including nearby landmarks or access details
  • A phone number where you can be reached
  • What happened and what you can observe
  • How many people need assistance
  • Whether the person is responsive and breathing normally, if you can safely tell

Answer as clearly as you can. If you do not know something, say so rather than guessing.

While waiting for help

Keep yourself safe

Do not enter an unsafe area. Follow any safety instructions from emergency services.

Follow the dispatcher

Listen carefully and follow the instructions to the best of your ability.

Share important changes

Tell the dispatcher if the person’s condition or the situation changes.

Know how to reach help locally

Emergency numbers and procedures can vary by location. Learn how to contact emergency services where you live. If you are unsure which number to call during an emergency, seek help from a local authority or trusted emergency resource in advance.
