BLS Myths and Facts

Understand common misconceptions about emergency response and learning basic life support.

Home / Projects / Basic Life Support Awareness / Myths and Facts

Important: This page is for general awareness only. It does not replace recognized, hands-on training or emergency dispatcher instructions. In an emergency, contact local emergency services and follow the dispatcher’s guidance.

Myth: Only healthcare professionals can help in an emergency

Fact: Members of the public may be able to help by contacting emergency services, following dispatcher instructions, and using skills learned through recognized training.

Myth: Reading about BLS is the same as being trained

Fact: Awareness materials can build understanding, but practical skills require appropriate hands-on training and practice.

Myth: An AED is only for medical professionals

Fact: AEDs provide prompts to guide users. Learn how to use one through recognized training, and follow the device prompts and emergency dispatcher instructions in an emergency.

Myth: It is better to wait and see if a serious emergency improves

Fact: Suspected life-threatening emergencies need urgent attention. Contact local emergency services promptly and follow their instructions.
