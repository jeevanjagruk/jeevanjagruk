Basic Life Support Awareness

Understanding BLS

Basic Life Support (BLS) is a set of emergency-response skills intended to help someone experiencing a life-threatening emergency, such as cardiac arrest, until advanced care is available.

Why BLS awareness matters

In an emergency, recognizing that someone may need urgent help and contacting emergency services promptly are important first steps. People who receive practical training can learn how to respond safely and appropriately while help is on the way.

What does BLS awareness cover?

Recognizing an emergency

Understanding when a situation may require urgent medical help.

Getting help

Contacting local emergency services and following their instructions.

Learning practical skills

Taking a recognized, hands-on course to learn and practise relevant skills.

Awareness is not a substitute for training

This page provides general educational information only. It does not teach or certify BLS skills. For practical instruction, attend a recognized course led by a qualified instructor. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately and follow their guidance.
