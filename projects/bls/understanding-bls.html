Basic Life Support Awareness

Understanding BLS

Basic Life Support (BLS) refers to emergency-response skills that may help support a person experiencing a life-threatening emergency until advanced medical care is available.

Why BLS awareness matters

Emergencies can be frightening, and people may be unsure what to do. Recognizing a possible emergency, contacting local emergency services promptly, and following the dispatcher’s instructions are important steps. Practical training can help people learn how to respond safely and appropriately.

What does BLS awareness cover?

Recognizing an emergency

Understanding when someone may need urgent medical assistance.

Getting help

Contacting local emergency services and following their guidance.

Learning practical skills

Finding a recognized, hands-on course led by a qualified instructor.

Awareness is not a substitute for training

This page provides general educational information only. It does not teach or certify BLS skills. Practical skills should be learned and practised through recognized, hands-on training. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately and follow their instructions.
